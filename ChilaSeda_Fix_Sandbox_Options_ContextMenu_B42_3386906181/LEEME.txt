ChilaSeda - Hide Sandbox Options Context Entry (B42) 1.0.0

Requires the Workshop mod [B42] Sandbox Options (SandboxSettings) and loads after it.
This patch removes the Sandbox Options entry from every world-object right-click menu.
It does not change the mod's configurable hotkey or its opening behavior.

Enable this local mod alongside SandboxSettings and restart the game to load it.
