ChilaSeda - Any Wall Flags 3D Weight Fix (B42)

Sets DGVFlags flag and banner definition weights to 0.15 kg. The estimate
models a light 3x5 ft cloth flag and does not include a pole.

This component is included in ChilaSeda's combined Weight Fixes
package. DGVFlags and True Weight are optional. The script scans loaded item
definitions, changes matching items when DGVFlags is present, and otherwise
does nothing. Workshop files are unchanged.
