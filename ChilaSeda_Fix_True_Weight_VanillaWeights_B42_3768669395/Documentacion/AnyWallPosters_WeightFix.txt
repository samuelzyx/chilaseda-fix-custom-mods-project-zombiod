ChilaSeda - Any Wall Posters 3D Weight Fix (B42) 1.1.0

Any Wall Posters 3D (DGVPosters) defines 99 posters at 0.2 kg as Furniture.
This component sets loaded poster definitions and existing accessible items
to 0.05 kg.

It is included in ChilaSeda's Weight Fixes package.
DGVPosters and True Weight are optional; absent poster definitions and items
are skipped. Workshop files are unchanged.
